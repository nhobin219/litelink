**`litelink.follow` is now `litelink.snapshot`.** The old name keeps working and emits a
`DeprecationWarning`; it will be removed no earlier than 0.4. Update at your leisure — the call
is otherwise identical.

The name promised a subscription it never provided, and its own docstring had to open by saying
so: *"a snapshot, not a subscription"*. A name a docstring has to walk back is the wrong name.

---

**`snapshot(..., include_wal=False)` reads the archive alone, and it is much faster.** It skips
the litestream restore entirely — no replica, no scratch buffer, no subprocess. Measured against
S3 at 60–75 ms RTT: a 1.9 MB buffer took **7.2 s** to restore, of which transfer was ~0.2 s. The
rest is one LIST plan plus ~20 serial GETs, and the chain length grows with the log's **age**
rather than its size, because a slow stream accumulates LTX files on the sync interval however
few rows it holds. The same handle, archive-only, assembles in about a quarter of a second.

Reach for it when the last few minutes do not matter: historical and analytical reads over a
long window, where the freshness buys nothing and the seven seconds is the whole bill.

**It is a different view, not a faster route to the same one**, and two things follow that are
worth knowing before you use it.

*Staleness is the archive frontier, not the replication lag.* `sync` holds back a trailing run
still under `target_compact_size`, so on a quiet stream the frontier can lag indefinitely rather
than by the sync interval. `coverage()` reports what the handle can actually serve.

*A log whose archive has published nothing is refused* rather than served empty — that is the
ordinary state of a slow capture, and exactly when the buffer holds everything. The refusal
distinguishes two states that look identical from the archive alone: if a WAL replica is there
the log exists and the message says to retry with `include_wal=True`; if it is not, nothing at
that prefix names that log, and the message says so and prints the location both arguments
resolve to. Assuming the first would tell someone with a typo that their log is a slow
capture.

One cosmetic caveat: `coverage()` on an archive-only handle reports `wal_replication=False`
whatever the writer has set, because the archive does not record it.
